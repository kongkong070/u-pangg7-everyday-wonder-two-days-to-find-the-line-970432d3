One sentence, up to 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life.

## Entry

> My shadow on the wet pavement walked half a step ahead of me all the way home, as if it already knew the way and was only waiting for me to catch up.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only) validates the sentence against the gate — one sentence, 1–200 characters, not only white space — and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
```
